Peptides4Pets B2B Partner Agreement
Version: pets-b2b-v1.0-2026-10-28

Eligibility and role
This programme is for individually approved pet groomers, dog spas, mobile groomers and other expressly approved non-clinical pet-care businesses. Wellness approval does not confer Pets eligibility. Partners share clear research and product information, evidence limits and questions for a veterinarian. Partners must not diagnose, prescribe, dose or administer products, imply verified clinical results or endorsements, or make unsupported claims. Only approved share materials may be used.

Commission and attribution
An approved partner earns 20% of eligible commissionable Pets merchandise value after independently verified cleared payment. Shipping, non-eligible products and refunded eligible value do not earn commission. Only explicitly eligible peptide vials qualify; collagen, oral drops and BAC water are excluded. There is no customer discount or automatic higher commission tier.

A valid tracked Pets click or QR referral lasts strictly 30 days. An explicitly entered valid Pets code takes precedence over the latest valid click. Without cookie consent, a customer may enter the partner code at checkout. Every repeat order requires fresh valid attribution; there is no lifetime or recurring entitlement. One order can earn commission once. Wellness and Pets attribution, approvals, agreements and records remain separate.

Validation, refunds and payout
Verified payment creates pending commission, not an immediate payout. Commission becomes available after 14 days of validation. Verified cancellations, refunds and reversals reduce earnings proportionately to the refunded eligible merchandise; duplicate events do not duplicate earnings or adjustments. Refunds after payout are recorded as deductions against future available earnings. Reserved payouts must be reconciled before an adjustment is processed.

Payouts are monthly in ZAR by EFT, with a R500 minimum available balance; lower balances carry forward. A statement reserves eligible available earnings for a completed calendar month. A reservation is not evidence of a bank transfer. Payment is recorded only against independently verified payout evidence. Partners must provide any required payout verification through the approved secure process, not in public messages.

Records and acceptance
The authenticated dashboard shows only the partner's authorised programme records. Each order retains its accepted policy and rate snapshot. Partners must accept this version before referrals can earn under it. Updated terms require a new version and acceptance. Suspension stops new eligibility and does not erase historical records. Participation does not promise sales, income or clinical outcomes.
